Privacy Policy

Resume Lab for Mac · Last updated 21 July 2026

Resume Lab does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your Mac.

What the app does with your information

Resume Lab is a document editor. The resumes you create, including any names, contact details, work history, and photos you enter, are saved as ordinary files on your Mac in locations you choose. The app has no network features: it does not connect to the internet, does not require an account, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Because no data leaves your Mac, there is nothing for us to collect, sell, or share.

Purchases

Resume Lab is sold through the Mac App Store, and any purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details, or any other personal information from a purchase. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support email

If you contact us for support, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to respond to you. We do not add you to mailing lists or share your details with anyone.

Children

Resume Lab does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Resume Lab ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to MadebyMike Support.

The basics

What does Resume Lab run on?

Resume Lab is a native Mac app on the Mac App Store. It needs macOS 26 or later. One purchase includes every template, with no subscription.

Where are my resumes stored?

Each resume is an ordinary document file (.resume) saved wherever you choose on your Mac, so you can keep it in iCloud Drive, back it up or copy it like any other file.

How do I change template?

In the inspector on the right, open the Design tab and click Change… under Template. Your words, colour and page size come with you, so you can try all nineteen in seconds.

What is the ATS score?

The score in the ATS tab checks your content against resume best practices, such as action verbs, numbers and bullet length, and shows a breakdown by category. It is separate from the job match, and it is a guide rather than a promise about any company’s hiring software.

New in 1.2

How does the job match work? New

Open the ATS tab and paste the job ad into Job match. Resume Lab finds the skills, tools and phrases the ad asks for, tells the requirements from the nice-to-haves, and shows which ones your resume already covers. Click a missing keyword to go to where it belongs.

It all runs on your Mac. The ad is kept with your resume and is never uploaded anywhere.

How do I tailor my resume for different jobs?

Use variants. Click the menu at the top of the sidebar (it starts as Main) and choose New Variant… to make one. Each variant can show its own choice of roles and bullets, and has its own title, design and job ad. Anything you add appears in every variant, so they never drift apart.

Can I make one long page instead of separate pages? New

Yes. In the Design tab, turn on Continuous page. Your resume becomes a single page with no breaks, which suits sharing online. Turn it off to go back to A4 or US Letter pages.

Can I write my resume in another language? New

Yes. Choose Headings & Labels in the sidebar and type your own words for every heading and label a template prints, such as “Berufserfahrung” for Experience. Leave a field empty to keep the template’s word.

Why do my resume files show a preview in Finder? New

Resume files now show their real first page as their icon. Select one and press Space to see it in Quick Look without opening the app. If previews don’t appear straight after updating, open Resume Lab once, then reopen the Finder window.

Can new resumes start with my favourite template? New

Yes. Choose ResumeLab > Settings (⌘,) and pick the template, colour and page size every new resume starts with, and whether it starts as a continuous page. Each resume can still change its own design afterwards.

Exporting and printing

How do I export, share or print?

Click Export PDF in the toolbar. Resume Lab suggests a proper file name, like “Jane Doe - Resume.pdf”. Share and Print sit next to it, so you can send your resume straight to Mail, Messages or AirDrop, or print exactly what you export.

Can hiring software read my PDF?

Yes. Every export is real, selectable, searchable text, never a picture of a page, with clickable email and web links. For the strictest application portals, a single-column template such as Plain, Classic or Granite is the safest choice.

My resume looks slightly different since updating. Why?

Version 1.2 fixed templates that left details out. Broadsheet now prints your contact details, Bond your job title, nine templates your education notes and Onyx your links. Long entries move to the next page instead of splitting, and blank entries no longer leave stray punctuation. A few resumes gain a page as a result; trimming a bullet or two usually brings it back.

Privacy

What data does Resume Lab collect?

None. Resume Lab has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and nothing you type leaves your Mac. Read the privacy policy.

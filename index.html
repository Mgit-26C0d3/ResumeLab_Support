Frequently asked questions

What does Resume Lab run on?

Resume Lab is a native Mac app available on the Mac App Store. It requires macOS 26 or later.

Where are my resumes stored?

Resumes are saved as ordinary document files (.resume) wherever you choose on your Mac, and export as standard PDF. The app has no network features, so nothing you type ever leaves your machine.

What data does Resume Lab collect?

None. Resume Lab has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
